Featured AI work

On-device intelligence, shipped to enterprise sales teams.

Features I designed and built for the SAP Sales Cloud iOS app on iOS 26, powered by Apple's on-device Foundation Models, so sensitive customer conversations never leave the device.

Apple Intelligence · Speech · PDF iOS 26

In-meeting capture & AI summaries

Sales reps tap Record during a customer visit and see a live transcript, with pause, resume and cancel. When the meeting ends, the on-device Foundation Model turns the transcript into a structured summary that is saved to the visit as a PDF.

  • Brief2–4 sentence recap
  • SentimentHow the customer felt
  • KeywordsTopics & products
  • PeopleWho was mentioned
  • ActionsSuggested next steps

Privacy-first by design: explicit consent and microphone permission flows, availability checks for Apple Intelligence-capable devices, and a staged rollout behind a feature flag.

FoundationModelsSpeechSwiftUIPrivacy by designPDF export
Vision OCR · Foundation Models

AI business card scanner

Scan a card and get a ready-to-save lead, contact or account. Vision reads the text, then the on-device model extracts names, job title, company, phones, email, website and a structured address.

  • Upgraded to iOS 26 RecognizeDocumentsRequest with automatic language detection, falling back to older Vision APIs.
  • Multilingual: Japanese, Korean, Arabic (right-to-left) and Hindi, with script detection and locale-specific prompts.
