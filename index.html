01 · About

Engineer by craft, changemaker by heart.

I turn complex business problems into simple, delightful experiences, and I believe technology should do good for both customers and communities.

I started at SAP as a Scholar@SAP building native iOS apps, then moved into CI/CD and release automation for SAP's cloud products. Today I build the SAP Sales Cloud native iOS app, bringing Generative AI and Business Intelligence into real-world sales.

I'm a trained Product Security Expert, an alumna of the Cambridge Rising Women Leaders Program, and a long-time CSR leader. I'm based in Bangalore, often underwater.
